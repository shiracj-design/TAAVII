Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need a large following to apply? +
No minimum following required. We evaluate partners based on content quality, audience engagement, and alignment with the TAAVII brand — not just follower count.
How and when do I get paid? +
Commissions are tracked automatically by each affiliate platform and paid monthly via bank transfer or PayPal. Typical payment threshold is $50.
Do I need to hold inventory or ship products? +
Absolutely not. You share a link — the brand handles everything: inventory, shipping, returns, and customer service. Zero risk on your end.
Can I promote multiple brands at the same time? +
Yes. You can apply to as many brands in our portfolio as you like. There is no exclusivity requirement — the more you promote, the more you can earn.
Is this available internationally? +
Yes. Our affiliate partners are active worldwide. Commissions are available regardless of your location, including Haiti, Canada, France, and all other countries.
